# Category: filing | Case Filing & Registration

## How do I file a new revenue court case online?

To file a case online, log into your account, navigate to the Cases dashboard, and click on **File New Case**. Fill in the basic details, case location, and court details. Then upload the necessary PDF documents (such as petitions, identity proof) and click submit. You will receive a temporary filing number instantly.

## What documents are mandatory for filing a mutation case?

For land mutation cases, you must upload:

- Registered Sale Deed or Will copy
- Latest Khatauni (Bhu-Abhilekh)
- Identity proof of the applicant
- No-objection certificate (NOC) if applicable

All documents must be uploaded in clear PDF format with size less than 5MB.

# Category: tracking | Case Tracking

## How can I track the live status of my case?

You can track your case in two ways:

1. **Public Search**: Click 'Search Case' on the homepage and search by Case Number, CNR Number, or Party Name.
2. **Dashboard**: Log into your account to see the full list of your active cases, next hearing dates, and current stage of proceedings.

# Category: summons | Summons & Notices

## What should I do if I receive a digital Summons/Notice?

A digital Summons or Notice requires your immediate attention. Log into the portal and check the **Notices** tab under your case details. Download the PDF to view the scheduled date, time, and specific court room. You must appear personally or through a registered Advocate on the specified hearing date.

# Category: hearings | Hearings & Calendar

## How can I request an adjournment (postponement) of a hearing?

To request an adjournment, your registered Advocate must file an Adjournment Application online at least 24 hours before the scheduled hearing. The application must outline valid grounds (e.g., medical emergency, conflict in another court). The Presiding Officer (Judge) will review the application and decide whether to grant a new date.

# Category: orders | Orders & Judgments

## How do I download copies of interim or final orders?

Once the Presiding Officer signs and uploads an order, it becomes public. Go to your case detail view, open the **Orders** tab, and click the download icon next to the relevant order entry. Certified copies can also be requested digitally from the main dashboard.
