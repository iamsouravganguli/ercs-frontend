# Category: hearings | Hearings & Calendar

## As a Peshkar, how do I schedule and publish case hearings?

To schedule a hearing, search for the case, go to the **Hearings** tab, and click **Add Hearing**. Select the date from the calendar (ensuring it doesn't exceed the court's daily quota), select the hearing cause/purpose (e.g., Evidence, Arguments), and submit. This action automatically updates the daily cause list and notifies the parties via SMS/email.

# Category: orders | Orders & Judgments

## What is the procedure for Judges to draft, sign, and upload orders?

Judges can access the **Orders** tab of a case:

1. **Draft**: Create a draft order using the integrated rich text editor, or upload a Word document draft.
2. **Sign**: Link the DSC (Digital Signature Certificate) token, sign the finalized PDF order.
3. **Upload**: Once signed, click **Publish Order**. Published orders are automatically pushed to public tracking and case history timeline.

# Category: summons | Summons & Notices

## How are summons generated, approved, and dispatched by Clerks?

When a court orders summons issue:

1. Go to the **Summons/Notices** module, click **Generate Summons**.
2. Choose the party, verify address details, and auto-populate the summons template.
3. Submit for approval if required, then sign digitally.
4. Dispatch via the post integration (speed post tracking barcoded sheet) or direct digital delivery to the defendant's email/SMS inbox.

# Category: tracking | Case Tracking

## How do court officers record daily court proceedings and presence?

During live court sessions, the Peshkar or Clerk should use the **Daily Court Board** module. Here you can mark party attendance (Present, Absent, Represented by Advocate), record the short order/action of the day (e.g., Adjourned, Disposed, Summons Ordered), and specify the next hearing date. Saving the board syncs the status instantly across all systems.

# Category: filing | Filing & Registration

## How do Clerks review and scrutinize new online filings?

Clerks review filings from the **Awaiting Review** dashboard:

1. Open filing details, review uploaded documents, jurisdiction details, court fee payment verification.
2. If everything is correct, click **Accept Case** to generate the official Case Number.
3. If deficient, click **Object / Return Case** and select objection codes (e.g., Insufficient fee, Missing sale deed) to notify the advocate to rectify.
